## Version 1.2.0: nine motion clips and principles

Date: 30 October 2026. Baseline: `afc3d2b`. This is a subsequent update to the existing source package. It adds a directly routed principles/choreography guide, expands temporal reference analysis and visual QA, distinguishes spring physics from visible bounce, and coordinates the shared brief with Graphic Design. The guide covers staging, overlap, follow-through, holds, four distinct clocks, readable/usable/settled milestones, masks, typography, linked indicators, shared media, and surface assembly.
